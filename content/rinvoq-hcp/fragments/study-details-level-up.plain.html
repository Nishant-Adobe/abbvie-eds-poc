Study Details

Primary endpoint:

Proportion of patients achieving vIGA-AD 0/1 with ≥2-grade improvement at Week 16 in the H2H period.

Select ranked secondary endpoints:

  • EASI 75 at Week 16
  • Worst Pruritus NRS ≥4-point improvement at Week 16
  • EASI 90 at Week 16
  • EASI 100 at Week 16
  • vIGA-AD 0 at Week 16

Key inclusion criteria:

  • Adults (≥18 years) with moderate to severe AD (EASI ≥16, vIGA-AD ≥3, BSA ≥10%)
  • Candidates for systemic therapy
  • Inadequate response to topical therapies or when topicals were medically inadvisable

Baseline Characteristics

Patients were randomized 1:1 to receive RINVOQ 30 mg QD or DUPIXENT (300 mg Q2W after a 600 mg loading dose).

  • N=348 (RINVOQ n=174, DUPIXENT n=174)
  • Mean age: 34 years
  • Mean EASI: 29
  • Mean BSA: 46%
  • Mean WP-NRS: 7.2
